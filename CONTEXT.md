# tuanopoly.com — domain glossary

The site is a portfolio for Anh Tuan Nguyen, an AI-native developer. Its one job is to get a potential client to send an email.

## Terms

**Project**
One shipped tool with its own Live URL on a subdomain of tuanopoly.com. A Project is not a Project until it is live and usable by a stranger. Work in progress is "in progress", not a Project.

**Live URL**
The public address where a Project can be used. Always a real subdomain of tuanopoly.com, never a preview or platform-generated link.

**Build Log**
The short write-up attached to a Project. It says what the Project does, what the AI produced, and what was fixed or decided by hand. Starts as three bullets on the Project's card; may grow into its own page.

**Visitor**
Anyone reading the site. The Visitor the site is designed for is a client who might pay for a freelance build.

**Primary action**
The one thing the site asks a Visitor to do: email tuan@tuanopoly.com.
