AccentPal

Plugin in Figma review

A Figma plugin for UI designers. Paste one accent color, compare four pairings on a real layout, and write a complete light-and-dark palette to Figma variables. Every pairing passes WCAG AA.

  • What it does. Turns one color into twelve roles, both modes, with the contrast ratio next to every text color.
  • What the AI produced. The color engine and its tests, the Figma plugin, the licensing Worker, and the landing page.
  • What I decided by hand. The accent is never altered, the pricing, and a default accent chosen on contrast and color-blindness math.
